# AI Usage & Declaration

## Overview

EARTH-616 was developed by our team with the assistance of artificial intelligence tools. We used AI to accelerate software development, support research and problem-solving, and produce selected creative assets for our project presentation.

AI served as an assisting tool throughout development. The project concept, technical decisions, implementation direction, scientific data selection, integration, and final validation were handled and reviewed by our team.

## AI Tools and Their Applications

| AI Tool | How We Used It |
|---|---|
| Google Gemini | Assisted with research, brainstorming, technical guidance, and understanding Earth science concepts and datasets. |
| GitHub Copilot | Assisted with code suggestions, implementation, debugging, and development of application features. |
| ChatGPT | Assisted with project planning, technical problem-solving, code guidance, documentation, and presentation preparation. |
| Google Flow | Generated selected cinematic video clips used in our project presentation. |

## AI Usage in Development

AI coding assistants were used to support the development of the interactive 3D Earth experience, scientific visualizations, timeline interactions, and data sonification features.

AI-generated code suggestions were reviewed, tested, and adapted by our team before being incorporated into the application.

AI tools also assisted with research and understanding scientific concepts. They were not treated as authoritative scientific sources.

## Scientific Data Integrity

EARTH-616 uses publicly available scientific observations and visualization resources from NASA and NOAA.

The scientific measurements used by the application originate from their respective data providers. We did not use AI-generated measurements as substitutes for the original scientific datasets.

AI tools assisted with development and interpretation guidance; scientific data attribution remains with the original providers.

## AI-Generated Media Disclosure

Google Flow was used to generate selected cinematic visual clips for the project presentation.

AI-generated video content is identified with a visible disclosure watermark. Any additional AI-generated audio or visual assets are also disclosed where applicable.

No AI-generated media is intended to represent authentic NASA footage, actual scientific observations, or real project demonstrations.

## Human Responsibility and Verification

Our team takes responsibility for the final implementation, scientific context, accuracy of the information presented, and overall project output.

AI-generated suggestions and materials were treated as assistance rather than unquestioned authority. We reviewed the outputs and made the final decisions regarding their use.

## Declaration

We acknowledge the use of artificial intelligence tools in the development and presentation of EARTH-616. We believe AI can support creativity, experimentation, and technical productivity when used responsibly and transparently.

The final project represents our team's ideas, decisions, implementation efforts, and collaborative work.
